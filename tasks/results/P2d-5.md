- `h : m.heap.cell? a = some (.suspended e env)`

1. `((whnf (n + 1) a).run).run m = (Except.ok v, m₃)`

3. `∀ k, ((whnf (k + 1) a).run).run m₃ = (Except.ok v, m₃)`（任意の正の fuel で再強制しても `ok v`、Machine は不変）
